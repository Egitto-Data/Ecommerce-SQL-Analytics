# Business Questions and KPI Definitions

## 1. Sales Performance

### Business Questions
- What is the total revenue?
- How many orders were placed?
- What is the average order value?
- How many units were sold?

### Key Performance Indicators

- **Revenue:** Total sales value, calculated as quantity multiplied by unit price.
- **Total Orders:** Number of distinct orders.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Total revenue divided by the number of orders.
- **Average Selling Price (ASP):** Total revenue divided by total units sold.
- **Units Per Transaction (UPT):** Total units sold divided by total orders.

## 2. Customer Analysis

### Business Questions
- Which customers generate the highest revenue?
- Which customers have placed multiple orders?
- Which customers have never placed an order?
- When did each customer place their first and last order?

## 3. Repeat Purchase Rate

**Definition:**

Repeat Purchase Rate = Repeat Customers / Purchasing Customers × 100

A repeat customer is a customer who has placed more than one distinct order.

Customers who have never ordered are excluded from the calculation.

## 4. Monthly Sales Trends

### Business Questions
- How does revenue change over time?
- Is revenue increasing or decreasing?
- How does the current month compare with previous periods?

**Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth:**

(Current Month Revenue - Previous Month Revenue) / Previous Month Revenue × 100

**Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth:**

(Current Month Revenue - Same Month Previous Year Revenue) / Same Month Previous Year Revenue × 100

## 5. Customer Segmentation

Customers are grouped based on their total revenue.

- High Revenue
- Mid Revenue
- Low Revenue

The SQL implementation uses NTILE(3), which creates relative groups rather than fixed revenue thresholds.

## 6. RFM Analysis

RFM stands for:

- **Recency:** Days since the customer's last order.
- **Frequency:** Number of distinct orders.
- **Monetary:** Total revenue generated by the customer.

RFM helps identify valuable customers and customers who may need attention.

## 7. Product Analysis

### Business Questions
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the most units?
- What is the estimated gross profit by product?

Estimated gross profit is calculated using product cost and selling price. It excludes other operating expenses.

## 8. Market Basket Analysis

Market Basket Analysis identifies products that frequently appear in the same order.

- **Support:** Percentage of all orders containing both products.
- **Confidence:** Percentage of orders containing Product A that also contain Product B.
- **Lift:** Compares the co-occurrence of two products with the overall frequency of Product B.

These metrics indicate associations, not causation.

## 9. Data Quality

The project checks:
- Table row counts.
- Invalid quantities and prices.
- Orders without order items.
- Customers without orders.
- Missing product references.

## Limitations

The repository contains SQL scripts and documentation only. No dataset or data-loading script is included. Query results require data to be available in the defined SQL Server tables.
